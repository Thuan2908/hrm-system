# Product Scope

## In Scope
1. Admin & RBAC
2. Employee profile & organization
3. Attendance & OT
4. Leave/absence/resignation requests
5. Payroll & payslip
6. HR/management reports
7. Audit log
8. Backup/restore procedure
9. Employee self-service
10. Bank payment export

## Out of Scope baseline
- AI dự báo nghỉ việc
- FaceID mobile attendance
- Recruitment ATS đầy đủ
- Performance management nâng cao

Các mục ngoài scope chỉ được đưa vào sau Change Request/ADR.
